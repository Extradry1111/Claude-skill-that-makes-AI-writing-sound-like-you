# 7-day template launch playbook

| Day | Do this | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Pick an idea that scores 18 or more. Build BOT.md, skills and routines | `/template-forge` |
| 0 | Use it yourself for a day. Fix whatever annoys you | · |
| 1 | Scrub it, run `python3 scripts/validate.py`, then Share as template and create a public link | `/template-scrub` |
| 1 | Launch post with the share link, the paid partnership label and a screen recording | `/template-launch` |
| 2 | Quote-post a real output from today | `/template-launch` |
| 3 | Reply to 5 big accounts in the niche with something useful. No link in the reply | · |
| 4 | Quote-post a user question and the bot's answer | `/template-launch` |
| 5 | Ship v2 with the most-asked feature | `/template-forge` |
| 6 | Changelog quote-post | `/template-launch` |
| 14 | First payout window. Log it | `/rewards-log` |

## Rules of thumb

- **Launch on a news day.** For Liftoff that means the day before a Starship flight, when your niche has the most attention.
- **Proof, not promises.** Every post needs a screenshot or a screen recording of the bot actually working.
- **One template, many posts.** The template is the product and the posts are the distribution.
- **Don't spam your own followers into it.** People who copy it and never come back don't count for much.
